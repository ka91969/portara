Villa Orestiàs

Crafted with unwavering dedication to authentic Cycladic architectural traditions while incorporating refined contemporary elements, this residence epitomizes the quintessence of Mediterranean luxury living. Villa Orestiàs appeals to discerning travelers in pursuit of tranquil rejuvenation, situated a mere 600 meters from Plaka beach's pristine shoreline, 4 kilometers from Agia Anna's renowned water sports haven, and a leisurely 15-minute drive from the historic allure of Naxos Town. The villa's thoughtful architectural design maximizes the spectacular dual aspects, with expansive windows framing seascapes on one side and mountain vistas on the other. The interior spaces are arranged across a sophisticated maisonette configuration, offering three elegantly appointed bedrooms, each featuring private balconies or terraces that extend the living experience into the natural surroundings. Moreover, three bathrooms incorporate premium fixtures and sustainable amenities, ensuring comfort and convenience throughout your stay.

Villa Dryàs

On the enchanting Greek island of Naxos, Villa Dryàs presents a private haven of refined elegance where luxury meets authentic Cycladic charm. This exclusive residence offers discerning travelers a sophisticated retreat featuring views of the Aegean Sea, the surrounding mountains and the meticulously maintained gardens. Villa Dryàs features three elegantly appointed bedrooms designed to provide ultimate comfort. The first two bedrooms offer a double bed each, while the third bedroom provides both a double bed and a single bed—perfect for families or groups seeking versatile sleeping arrangements. The living room includes a comfortable sofa bed for 2 additional guests.

Villa Naiàs

Villa Naiàs presents an exquisite two-storey residence of thoughtfully designed living space. This exceptional property combines contemporary design elements with traditional Cycladic architectural influences to create a sophisticated island retreat experience. The villa accommodates up to 8 guests in refined comfort, making it ideal for families or small groups seeking a premium Naxian holiday. The residence features a meticulously conceived floor plan distributed across two distinct levels. Climate comfort is ensured through individually controlled air conditioning throughout the property. Adjacent to the living space, a comprehensively equipped kitchen provides everything required for culinary exploration, featuring a full suite of premium appliances including an oven, refrigerator, coffee machine, electric kettle, and toaster. The convenient inclusion of a laundry machine addresses practical needs during extended stays. A versatile sofa converts to a comfortable double bed, offering additional sleeping accommodation when required.
